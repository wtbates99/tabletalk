main.customers|Customer accounts. region may be null; segment is consumer, small_business, or enterprise.|id:I[PK]|name:S|email:S|region:S|segment:S|signup_date:D|active:B

main.order_items|Purchased products. Extended line revenue is quantity * unit_price - discount.|id:I[PK]|order_id:I[FK:orders.id]|product_id:I[FK:products.id]|quantity:I|unit_price:N|discount:N
